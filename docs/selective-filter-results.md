# Selective filtered ANN probe

This report is generated by `make selective-filter`. It deliberately chooses 
real Wikipedia categories containing at least `LIMIT` matching chunks but only 
a small fraction of the 3,940-vector corpus. That distinguishes ANN/filter 
underfill from the trivial case where fewer than `LIMIT` rows are eligible.

- Query: `neural network training`
- LIMIT: **10**
- Corpus vectors: **3,940**
- Categories tested: **12**
- Eligible-chunk window: **10–50**
- `mhnsw_ef_search`: **20, 200, 800**
- Repetitions: **5** measured after **1** warm-up run(s)

## Finding

No filtered-ANN underfill was observed in this controlled selectivity window: every tested category had at least LIMIT eligible chunks and the indexed query returned LIMIT rows at every tested `mhnsw_ef_search` value. The exact-top-k overlap still quantifies approximation under the selective predicates.

Underfilled categories: **ef=20: 0/12 · ef=200: 0/12 · ef=800: 0/12**.

Mean overlap with index-ignored exact top-k: **ef=20: 100.0% · ef=200: 100.0% · ef=800: 100.0%**.

The index-ignored mode is used only as the exact comparison baseline and the 
vector index is restored in a `finally` block after the experiment.
